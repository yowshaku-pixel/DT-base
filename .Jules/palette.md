## 2025-02-14 - Semantic Interactive Wrappers
**Learning:** Using generic HTML elements like `div` or non-semantic Framer Motion `motion.div` containers for interactive actions causes accessibility issues. They are not focusable by default, do not support native keyboard activators (like Space or Enter), and lack proper ARIA role definitions. Converting them to semantic elements (such as `motion.button` with `type="button"`, focus ring outlines, and `aria-label` descriptors) immediately restores full keyboard accessibility and screen-reader support without requiring manual key listeners.
**Action:** Always prefer `motion.button` over `motion.div` for clickable Framer Motion wrappers, ensuring proper focus indicator styles, `type="button"`, and explicit `aria-label` attributes.
